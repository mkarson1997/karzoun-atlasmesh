## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-05

### Added

- Initial Go control-plane foundation.
- TTL service leases and health-aware weighted resolution.
- Cluster membership heartbeats.
- At-least-once job queue with retries, lease fencing and dead-letter state.
- REST API, Prometheus metrics, structured logging and graceful shutdown.
- Race-detector CI across supported Go toolchains.
- CodeQL, pinned govulncheck and Dependabot security automation.
- Rootless container image and cross-platform release binaries with SHA-256 checksums.
- Architecture, security, contribution, citation and ownership documentation.
